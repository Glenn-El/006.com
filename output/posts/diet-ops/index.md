My kids tease me that I put the word Ops after everything. Ops is military slang for Operations. There is so much information, often conflicting, available today on what you should or should not eat, making it extremely difficult to navigate. I've studied this extensively, consulted with multiple experts, and tried many different approaches throughout my life and continue to learn what works or doesn't work for me.

### Basic Terms & Concepts

In order to determine your personal Diet Ops you must first understand a few key terms and concepts.

- **Calorie** - Form of energy measurement. Used for energy or stored.

- **Calorie Balance** - Calories In (consumed) equal the calories Out (burned/used). Tissue weight will remain stable (the same).

- **Calorie Deficit** - Consuming fewer calories than your body expends. ALWAYS results in weight loss.

- **Calorie Surplus** - Eat more than you burn. Your body stores these extra calories and this ALWAYS results in weight gain.

### Priority #1 - Determine your Calorie Balance

The simplest method is to use a scale and weigh your bodyweight a few times a week to see how it is trending. A best practice is to weigh yourself the same time every day typically first thing when you get up prior to eating your breakfast.

### 1-2 lbs per week = Ideal rate to gain or lose weight

Depending on your fitness goals you may be trying to lose, maintain, or gain weight. If your goal is to gain or lose weight, research indicates that 1-2 lbs per week is the ideal rate or pace to set to achieve your goals. A pound of tissue is composed of 3,500 calories. To build a pound of tissue, 3,500 calories must be consumed in excess of your calorie balance. To lose a pound, a deficit of 3,500 calories is required. So divide 3,500 calories by 7 days in a week and you get the following rule of thumb: **Adjust 500 - 1,000 calories/day to achieve the best results**.

### Priority #2 - Dial in your Macronutrients

**Macronutrients** are a substance required in relatively large amounts for the normal growth and development of an organism. Macronutrients for animals include fat, carbohydrate, and protein.

**Proteins** are the most important dietary macronutrient as muscle is made out of it. Protein intake is essential to build new muscle and save your existing muscle. _About 1g of protein per pound of body weight is best for most people._

The primary role of **carbohydrates** (carbs) is to provide energy for your workouts and for recovery. Higher carbs should be eaten with higher workout volumes and daily activity levels.

Healthy/**essential fats** in small amounts are required for survival as they are the basis for many hormones and chemical messengers in your body.

### Priority #3 - Nutrient Timing

Nutrient timing is all about when/how frequently you take in your nutrients and its relationship to activity.

| **Protein** | **Whey** (stays in your system <1 hour) - good for during or after workout   **Whole foods** like chicken breast or fish (stay in your system for several hours)   **Casein** (stays in your system for 7 hours) - good for before bed   \>3 meals per day is good, <2 = hard to supply your body with its protein needs   Note: Some people have allergies to milk and must find alternative protein sources. |
| --- | --- |
| **Carbohydrates** | On high training volume periods it is _good to increase your carb intake before, during, and after training_.   **Fiber** is a non-digestible carb that is good for your overall health. They are _slow to digest and should be kept out of your diet around the time of your workout_. |
| **Fat** | _Fat should not be consumed during your workout and be limited before and after your workout._   Fat helps to extend nutrient absorption between eating periods. |

### General principles

- Eat healthy food sources that are fresh and not processed as much as possible.

- Read the nutrient labels on foods carefully to see what all the ingredients that go into the product.

- Be sensitive to how your body feels and responds to different foods and adjust your diet accordingly.

- I know that I am allergic to some foods or ingredients and avoid them diligently as I know if I do not then I will suffer the consequences.

- Drink plenty of water throughout the day as being hydrated is important to your overall health.

Eating healthy is a lifetime pursuit so do not be discouraged if your progress towards your goals is not happening at the pace you want it to.
