> Commit your works to Yahweh  
> And your plans will be established.
> 
> Proverbs 16:3

Whether you are applying for your first job, or making a career transition it can be a daunting proposition. Here are some job hunting strategies that I have successfully used and I hope will aid you in the pursuit of your dream job.

## Step 1 - Personal Assessment & Goal Setting

![](images/x3rJvWMQXROsEl4vqR4-Bneg4vIT8nrqSM7PxHJKF5A0hc8g-GC8H5wnxs4iAUZBvDOYrwZ1UErt3ILbpPE8CtHa6g8SdOyV5eNoZXe9WbbjlgW8j0JZ_c49J7LSjxf1wZtw4zT6PQ=w2400)

- **Personal Assessment**
    - Honestly assess your skills, education, experience and interests
    
    - Take free career tests to see what types of work fit your personality best
        - [Monster.com: Best free career assessment tools](https://www.monster.com/career-advice/article/best-free-career-assessment-tools)
    
    - Get feedback from your friends and family on what your strengths and talents are

> This personal assessment is meant to be informative and not something that limits what you go after.

![](images/HEDfOH4NUUlvGScu0UhOgQX6QAPdigLn9yMMhh5owXt64uqnZuagO_ai5sva0Jz_w-FF0kAyu8klOzzFM4ppkPcRvCzFL-FY9nl9X0vJq20jX4HMu_-6KyUiJPVNkj4zzi4M3S712A=w2400)

- **Set Goals**
    - Pray that the Lord will give you wisdom and discernment about your job hunt and how it will honor and bring Him glory
    
    - What is your dream job and how can you take steps to get there?
    
    - Does the job you want have education/certification prerequisites?
    
    - For me I was in the tech industry and wanted to go back to my High School dream of working in the entertainment industry
    
    - Set short (in the next year), mid (2-3 years), and long term goals (5+ years)
    
    - My short term goal was to jump industries from tech to entertainment tech and then explore new opportunities as they arise to keep incrementally working towards my dream job
    
    - My mid term goal was then to then take my experience in entertainment tech to get to the company I wanted to work for
    
    - My long term goal was then within the company I wanted to work for to then have the job function/role I wanted to have
    
    - Some may be able to go straight into the job and company that they want to work for right away, others may have to take a longer journey of getting the education and experience they need to get to where they want to be.

## Step 2 - Industry Research

![](images/pqyB06bbA_dhzbo2Y523s4U8VP5Uvw9JrMmGF9YQDtP2H0mXDlnN5ToDqzOU141JnZdmK1uJ93T-famQgtLlspqphhK4h88L7oCpEcIJVMV0gogkJPn6j90evcfz3v4QyvoeSkYCpQ=w2400)

- Collect a list of all the major players in the industry you are interested in, where they are located, how they are doing financially and if they are hiring

- For me I wanted to work in the entertainment industry in a solid company that had a good track record and seemed to be an industry leader with good future growth opportunities

- Also a key criteria for me was location as my wife and I wanted to move to Southern California where we grew up

- Identify what the jobs/roles you are interested in and what they make
    - There are job salary tools that will help you know what particular job roles make in a given region

## Step 3 - Network & Apply to Jobs

The next step is to prepare your resume, cover letter, and update your LinkedIn profile. Never overstate your qualifications as it will come back and hurt you.

> Make sure your public social media accounts reflect a professional image and are desirable for a future employer.

![](images/cMKhtmL9F5drerYDFJUpmj1fQ1sIoTqQ09t4fo4og5sOt_JWGIFGXJGqmp9pg5gNVqZRa0rhZtadZA_dMYYWIZPUcsvBaZOOjSdmLzXud0StVEVnouq6tmHvMkFnmAwI3zyIuOhi5g=w2400)

- **Networking**
    - Reach out to **recruiters** on LinkedIn at the companies you are interested in joining
        - It is their job to network and connect with people that might be good fits for the companies they represent
    
    - Connect with **professionals** who are in the industry and in the companies you are interested in joining
        - Ask them questions like:
            - How they like working there
            
            - How they got hired
            
            - To please connect you with the right person in their company to help guide and provide insights on how you can start a career with their company
            
            - How should you prepare and position yourself for success
            
            - Are you a good fit for any current open positions?

![](images/QJeN-fWTemulsTWI8kSKch5SOIUe_7q1k_qTzWK--x-M5vfwLc2ie824sNnneyQOX7vpQQUeQoQzFc2ZfxuoxUN0QhFiweL5x2hFUZjX0fFHvpw1uSG7fyBn7RvkMH20vV6VpcXsqA=w2400)

- **Apply to jobs & track**
    - Create a spreadsheet and track all of the careers/job sites of the companies you are interested in
    
    - For each company you will most likely have to create an online account with your resume
    
    - You can set up job searches that email you when new opportunities come up
        - However I just checked the sites regularly to not miss any new job postings
    
    - For each job you apply to keep a copy of the job description as it will come in handy

Once you start applying to specific positions then write your recruiters and professionals that you networked with to let them know the specific job postings you applied to and ask them if they could give you any guidance or insight into these positions and see if you would be a good fit.

> You must be patient and have endurance.

It often takes months if not a full year before you hear back from some companies. In my last job move it took three months of searching and applying. I have a friend where she didn't hear from a company for a full year.

## Step 4 - Interviewing & Negotiating

You finally got a response on your application and they want to talk with you!

![](images/MkEeB9LnZwb4w4El8lWFtyplcMrQ8P3nfUCUN9boAn49-4HjR2IbFkrI_60m--vO6utYE3D6laZr9wiRetr6_MxJQzetNxTQ5cUi3Z4vZQHuwN9H12NXBbW25L-mPlUEtcaozdyNNg=w2400)

- **Interviewing Prep**
    
    - Often your first interview will be a phone interview with the recruiter
    
    - Be ready for the interview by re-reading the job posting for the position you applied for
    
    - Make sure that you can clearly articulate how your skills and experiences line up with the job description
    
    - Practice answering tough interview questions with your friends and family
    
    - When I interview I keep a pad of paper ready to write down the questions they ask me and jot down the key points I want to make when answering
    
    - Do not be afraid of asking clarifying questions if the question is not clear to you
        - Be sure you understand the question and then give your succinct and well thought out answer
    
    - Be prepared with questions that you might have

> One of the best questions that I ever heard from a person I was interviewing was when she asked me how she did on the interview and how she could improve.

- **Interviewing Tips**
    - Be attentive and show your enthusiasm
    
    - For me I am very animated and have learned that I need to sometimes sit on my hands so that I am not just waving them around like crazy which is a major distraction
    
    - Speak clearly, with confidence, and make eye contact
    
    - Remember that this is a two way conversation where they are trying to figure out if you would be a good fit for their company
        - You should be doing the same to see if it is a good fit for you
    
    - If they do ask you about your salary requirements you must be ready with an honest answer and if challenged be able to back it up with facts based on your research
        - This typically happens a bit later in the process when they like you and want to make an offer
    
    - For video conference call interviews or in person interviews be sure to dress nicely and be punctual

![](images/Ydn5Fz5tIqMI6oViUZ792ZYSvzglKyYNfqeLxDMw7ZehQ6vKw9vBpUfN5W5NTfbZD9rXMAyjH4drD8Pa_E6iuedomYIfVeGohdZdcYVpp7k2jxiFW5VC8gntzNv2AA6T3C4TNwaPOg=w2400)

You made it through all your interviews and now they are making their offer to you for the position!

- **Negotiating Tips**
    
    - What I have found is that some companies will try to low ball you in terms of the salary
    
    - This is where you must be ready and have done your research and find out what these positions are being paid in the region
    
    - It is often the case that negotiating for your pay at the start of your job is the only time this will happen
    
    - Know your salary requirement and be able to articulate it succinctly and clearly!
    
    - If the company wants you they will counter offer and try their best to meet your salary requirements

## Final Thoughts & Encouragement

Life is a journey where we will face many challenges and setbacks. There will be victories and defeats. You will make mistakes along the way. Learn from them and move on. Do not be afraid to go after your dreams. Pray every step of the way.

> Rejoice always, pray without ceasing, give thanks in all circumstances; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you.
> 
> 1 Thessalonians 5:16-18
