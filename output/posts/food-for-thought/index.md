> But solid food is for the mature, who because of practice have their senses trained to discern both good and evil.
> 
> Hebrews 5:14

I have been and continue to be a stubborn and slow learner when it comes to seeing life informed by God’s truth and perspective, and having it shape my thinking and behavior. The following devotional is one that I first shared at a Bible Study group that my family was a member of where our motto was "You don't have to be hungry to eat". But that is a story for another time...

### **When I was 11 years old**

My mom had a couple of friends that she had known for many years come over to our house in Mission Viejo, California for a fancy dinner. My mom's friends were both in the broadcast industry and lived in Beverly Hills, California, the lady was a beautiful Japanese television news anchor and her husband was a talent agent that had various celebrities that he managed.

My mom had prepared an elaborate steak dinner with all the fixins. We had the best china laid out for our distinguished guests. I was excited about the food and meeting these important people.

When we sat down to dinner I immediately chanted that I wanted the biggest piece of steak. I was hungry and only thought of myself and my desire. I had no clue about those around me and didn’t really care about them. My dad humored me and I remember the people at the table smiling, which I’m not sure how I interpreted it then as I didn’t really care, but at least I thought I was amusing them. They were probably thinking what a spoiled brat I was and smiling.

![](images/aqZf5qseKVS2zD6KrMVyt0K_TM6GP3EePDqFLrrg7VH-1zGzs927hhlNseKXr9vD2UBFPXg4l7_SoKfDRi-VOPYz2EM6rmF7d0NLtaEa-gMGYi_Twigxnk5HnG_CQlDxzoWvESGgog=w2400)

I got the biggest steak on my plate with all the fixings. I am hungry and ready to eat. Come on! So others get their food and we all start to dig in. I tried to cut my steak with my fork and knife. Unfortunately I didn’t understand that if you push down hard on the front of your plate that is towards you to cut a steak, it tips your plate over.

My very first attempt to cut the steak landed the entire plate of food in my lap. The beautiful steak, along with all the fixings were now squarely in my lap. Boy was I embarrassed! Again not thinking of others but really just focusing on me. Thinking of how dumb I looked and how the food was now ruined, my clothes dirty. I don’t think I realized how embarrassing that must have been for my parents. But the evening went on and was pleasant despite my incident.

### **Fast forward to now**

Where I am at now in my walk I believe that I at least have the ability to see how my actions might impact others. How they might honor or dishonor the Lord. I combat my childish impulses to gluttony, selfishness and try to at least follow some rules of common courtesy and etiquette. Offering others the first choice of the food for example. Praying out loud to thank the Lord for the meal and ask for His blessing. I have also learned a bit more about physics and gravity and the techniques to keeping my meal on my plate and successfully navigating it into my mouth without mishap.

But seriously I only used this story of the steak incident to illustrate just how small of a step I have taken over all these years to think first about God’s teaching and behave in a manner that honors Him and His commandments.

### **Honest self reflection**

As I look at my life, too often it is characterized by the following characteristics:

- How much I just think about me and my need for instant gratification

- How I look at ways that I can avoid pain and maximize pleasure

- How I lust after and place value in things and fun events versus putting my very best effort to align to God’s will for eternal benefit

- How I often do not prioritize relationships and in sharing the truth with the lost

- How I spend too much time in diversion and fun, instead of seeing the urgent need and suffering of those around me and doing something about it

My desire is to not be so focused on myself and my immediate needs and desires. I wish to truly serve those in my family, church, and community. I want the motivation behind these action to not be to seek man's esteem or for how it can benefit me but rather to faithfully serve and obey the Lord. It really has been hard for me to have the right perspective on things. God’s perfect perspective. God’s amazing example in the life here and in His everlasting ministry. How He had and has the power to do whatever He wanted/wants and chose to humble himself to be a human to show us the way. To show us His true nature and  be an example of it. To continue to reveal to us the eternal truth that from His perspective has always been and always will be. I think about how patient He has been, how loving, how faithful.

### **I am a work in progress**

A verse that really struck me recently in my bible reading is as follows:

> Therefore, since we have so great a cloud of witnesses surrounding us, let us also lay aside every encumbrance, and the sin which so easily entangles us, and let us run with endurance the race that is set before us, fixing our eyes on Jesus, the author and perfecter of faith, who for the joy set before Him endured the cross, despising the shame, and has sat down at the right hand of the throne of God.
> 
> Hebrews 12:1-2

I always had a very simplistic view of this verse. I would simply think that Jesus was really looking forward to getting back to heaven and be done with the very very painful task that He had set before Him.

I think that I have a bit more insight now about the depth of meaning in that verse when I try with my feeble brain to understand it from God’s perspective. How everything He did and continues to do is reflective of His perfect will and character. How He loves His bride which is the Church which is made up of believers. How He planned and purposed to do everything to bring His Father glory.

![](images/youngluke.png)

### **Questions for reflection**

- Do I have that "joy set before me" perspective on my tasks and chores in life, in my duties as a father, husband, employee, or ministry servant?

- Do I really even understand what kind of character our Lord has to do what He did and continues to do, and what is yet to come?

- Do I really live my life with that perspective of His sacrifice?  Of the perspective of finally meeting the Lord face to face and having to give an account?

- Do I have a fear, an awe at all of Him that has the power to save or cast people into hell forever?

- Do I live my life in glad obedience to the King of Kings? Or am I more concerned about myself and how others might think of me if I tell them about my hope and joy or even simply ask them to come to church or read the bible.

Just some food for thought and reflection. Steak anyone?
