This post explores how I continue to live out my faith in Hollywood one day at a time. The story begins with taking a heart check.

### **Is the entertainment industry for you?**

> Every way of a man is right in his own eyes, but the Lord weighs the heart.
> 
> Proverbs 21:2

Before setting out to pursue a career in the entertainment industry I strongly suggest that you do a heart check on your attitude and motivations. Before I became a Christian and follower of Jesus Christ this was simple. I wanted fortune and glory and the praise and adoration of people. However as a Christian I check my motivations daily to see if they are to honor God or self seeking.

You have to ask yourself is a career in entertainment for you. This applies to any vocation you pursue. There are numerous practical implications as well including where you would work and how it would impact your family and loved ones.

I focused my job search in the entertainment industry primarily at animation studios as they were for the most part family oriented entertainment companies that produce PG or G rated films and shows.

### **Spiritual Discipline**

Having spiritual discipline isn't just applicable to your work but to everything you do in your life. The following is a list of a few areas that are applicable to living out your faith at your work:

- Guard your tongue and be in the word daily

- The Lord is your boss so pursue excellence

- How you live is your testimony before a watching world

- Be in a discipleship relationship where you have an accountability partner

- Be a part of a local Church body and serve

- Be in a small bible study group

- Preach the gospel to yourself daily

### **Spiritual Maturity**

![](images/arthur.png)

> Put on the whole armor of God, that you may be able to stand against the schemes of the devil.
> 
> Ephesians 6:11

The process of maturing spiritually is a gradual one over time. Some of the areas that have been particularly challenging during my career have been:

**Balancing priorities**

- Family

- Church

- Career

**Managing stress**

- Saying No

- Turn off your work

- Health and fitness

I have come a long way in being able to better balance my priorities and manage stress in my life. However I'm definitely a work in progress and have to remain diligent in this.
