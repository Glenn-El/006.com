### **What is motivation?**

According to the Oxford English Dictionary motivation is a noun that means "a reason or reasons for acting or behaving in a particular way."

### **Why is it important?**

Your reason or reasons for acting or behaving in a particular way impact every area of your life. Every day, moment by moment, from when you wake up in the morning to when you lay down at night, you have to make decisions about what you are going to do with the time you have. Having a proper understanding and mastery of your motivations are essential to achieving life goals, dreams, and aspirations.

### **Psychological approach to motivation**

I recently watched a thought provoking video from Dr. Jordan B. Peterson that provided interesting insights into achieving maximal motivation. The video clip below is from a class lecture he did where Dr. Peterson was preparing his students to participate in a future authoring exercise. In this exercise he wanted the students to outline their desired future and also the place that they would end up if everything fell apart.

What I took away from Dr. Peterson's video was that in addition to having a _voluntary goal_ that you choose versus having one chosen for you it is helpful to combine that motivation to _achieve_ that goal with wanting to _avoid_ the worst case scenario where you do not achieve the goal.

> Be more afraid of not pursuing your goals than you are of pursuing them.
> 
> Dr. Jordan B. Peterson

Dr. Peterson explained that with humans there is an element of fear where people fear that they will not be able to achieve their goal which weakens them. To overcome this weakness Dr. Peterson asserts that you should combine your pull and push motivations to maximize your motivation. The formula for this is shown below:

#### Pull Motivation + Push Motivation = Maximal Motivation

- **Pull Motivation** = the desire to achieve a goal in front of you, your **approach systems pulling you forward**

- **Push Motivation** = the fear of the undesirable outcome behind you, your **anxiety chasing you and pushing you forward**
