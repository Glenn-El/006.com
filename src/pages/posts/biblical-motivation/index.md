### **What is Motivation?**

According to the Oxford English Dictionary motivation is a noun that means "a reason or reasons for acting or behaving in a particular way."

### **Why is it important?**

Your reason or reasons for acting or behaving in a particular way impact every area of your life. Every day, moment by moment, from when you wake up in the morning to when you lay down at night, you have to make decisions about what you are going to do with the time you have. Having a proper understanding and mastery of your motivations are essential to achieving life goals, dreams, and aspirations. For a Christian your motivations are incredibly important as everything you think and do is understood by God, and either honors or dishonors Him, or brings Him joy or does not.

### **Biblical understanding of motivation**

In the area of motivation the bible has a great deal to say. To start with God is incredibly interested in our motivations, the why behind what we do. The bible tells us that our motivation should not be for selfish gain or to seek the approval of men but rather our motivation should come from a desire to obey and honor God.
