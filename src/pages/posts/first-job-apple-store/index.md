> Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the inheritance as your reward. You are serving the Lord Christ.
> 
> Colossians 3:23-24

When I started working it was another big life milestone. I had done little jobs before helping out at my Uncle's restaurant as a dishwasher or doing small construction projects but my first job was at the Apple Computers store in El Toro, California. I was 17 years old and would work part time in the evenings as a Sales and Service Technician. Basically the original Geek Squad. At the time Apple had the Apple IIe personal computer and was just introducing the Apple Macintosh. It was a big deal back then to introduce a computer that had a Graphical User Interface (GUI), used a mouse, and was so portable and innovative.

It was a great time to be working for Apple as they were on the verge of introducing a completely new type of computer. For the Super Bowl in 1984 Apple ran the following advertisement which was directed by Ridley Scott and is still seen as one of the greatest advertisements ever.

I would ride my bike to and from work wearing my dress shirt and tie and help answer customer's questions, keep the warehouse tidy, build out computers to the customer specifications, and help out in the service lab testing and repairing computers. Sometimes I would be asked to pick up shipments from the distribution center and would drive the company van.

It was a great first job that I think I made around $3.35/hour which was minimum wage back then. I was and continue to be very interested in computers and have made a living working in the Technology industry. I have fond memories of working at Apple.

You learn a lot of valuable life lessons when working in a store about how to interact with your managers, co-workers, and customers. How everything you do and say is being observed and interpreted by others. I'm thankful and praise the Lord for how much he has blessed me with the skills and abilities to earn a living and provide for my family.
