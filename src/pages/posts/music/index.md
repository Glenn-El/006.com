> Finally, brothers, whatever is true, whatever is dignified, whatever is right, whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, whatever is commendable, if there is any excellence and if anything worthy of praise, consider these things.
> 
> Philippians 4:8

Music is a gift from God that I have enjoyed all my life. My tastes have changed over the years and I enjoy listening to so many different types of music genres. I have also played the clarinet in band and orchestra and piano. I don't have a great voice but I do love to sing and worship the Lord as well. There is such a connection to my emotions when I sing or listen to music. This post focuses on the top 10 albums that influenced me growing up.

## #1 - Star Wars

![](images/Ow4So5El8qqsxjayr95Mdhc2XWH3T33aFaOiCIbSMSpRum4J8oWraRCRxiJBMK21AI6g9cn8CQxE8B52HBDIt5vFus87iichHv_-ZiNJMT2LJlHzZSPfdHIWQBB9mU_svokbUGJgQA=w2400)

I must start with Maestro John Williams as he is so gifted that this whole list could be filled with his work. I saw Star Wars in 1977 when it came out. My sister took me to see it and I had no idea what the movie was supposed to be about. I was 11 years old and you can only imagine how much seeing that Movie and hearing the wonderful music had an impact on me. I went home and bought the album and would listen to it over and over. I probably played the album four times a day for a year. I knew every beat and could hear the London Symphony Orchestra players breath and bump their music stands, and even in one song I think I hear someone sigh. I absolutely love this album.

## #2 - The Police - Synchronicity

![](images/Dyz-JHhi2drh73iFTHPwKVP9X89txe0R09dwuT7fgkgkjGUDb1fQVb90C8NcwLZ2sPzu7lniExSmGoFA0XlQnD8r73LLdxeRDTIZjfrwxVhvIwFpoeBvyUG9Fb6wuUTANT8YRABdWg=w2400)

I listened to this album over and over as a teenager and have enjoyed these songs my whole life. During a bible study greeting time my pastor asked as an ice breaker question if we could be like any singer/musical performer in history who would we be. It was a hard question but I chose Sting as he has had such an amazing career and is still going strong. All of the members of The Police are gifted and talented.

## #3 - Michael Jackson - Thriller

![](images/7TGAKCsxhUa9ep2kVG1_D2_Zos-2RFjrMbW9xfnngRxoqracjoP2WlvpEoOWjdp_FOanXTEfrFg6i9lW8pBX-IQq7HKS2mLGvWC2FO5vKmy0a7g2N9zwxZkRZBJ13PNjJw8OXJKvwQ=w2400)

This album came out in 1982 when I was in High School and for me evokes so many great memories. Not everyone was or is a fan of Michael Jackson today. But for me his music and music videos made me want to dance, and had such an energy and spirit that spoke to me. His concerts were so popular I never was able to see him perform in person but I would have loved to have seen him in concert where his showmanship and flair was truly innovative and magical.

## #4 - Wagner: Tannhauser

![](images/r06EEiwUNb2UZwvKaHch8v2v-ZDIZZCwKbRJ_jsS1hqCvxb_hb57Z3V23-bEWenqdkg6feGErLRKC-SkDe7k6sk8HYktGSt5bi-w-WfQfZA35DxjqrBrcTMhBNpI0qXrLsS0EaePUw=w2400)

My father introduced me to classical music when I was young, he loved Sergey Rachmaninov who was an amazing pianist. Within Classical music the Romantic Period is my favorite and Richard Wagner is my favorite composer from that period. And if I had to pick one work of Wagner it would be Tannhauser. Many would pick The Ring as his crowning achievement but for me the passion and drama in Tannhauser are unparalleled. If you can’t handle opera singing (which for me was an acquired taste) then there are versions of Tannhauser Without Words that just features the beautiful music. When you listen to it you will see where many film composers get their inspiration.

## #5 - The Doors - The Best Of The Doors

![](images/Y3oXQh_el4ACO5DEBlizSZjUouFSOwPrRX3iKNyuDiedWDmoQkc4RHme6yzrkM-plEoYIZq4F2FAzUR_9HVDeNlt-gNLeRMXHFMa4VcrtdSZQxTyogjG14WPizgoXEd2aDpuRfd9GQ=w2400)

When it comes to American Rock and Roll one of my favorites is The Doors. I had heard their music growing up and it really got a hold of me when the movie The Doors came out and I started listening to all of their music. This album is a Best of compilation that has many of their greatest hits.

## #6 - Earth, Wind & Fire - Greatest Hits

![](images/QCUoFgjLQPwHoxSRdpXhThrBhm5fr3_Vhr1AbBMtTlPQri-Q_1fERNUCwAzSVCat4ruO0U8ykeWyGJ9pVZl-85up29PjoTOpcFqqw55Q5KHGRdl8WnBv4Zl6UBx02jZWFAJM_7nh5Q=w2400)

I loved the music from this band growing up and they have an impact and influence even to this day with their innovative style. It is hard to nail down their style but I found one online source that said they were a pop, soul, and jazz-fusion band. I can’t help but start to move and groove when I hear their tunes.

## #7 - Madonna - The Immaculate Collection

![](images/MUUDzqLxrL4f4wwC8x6uAxP4Qu-CRsDNfxj1_MRJ9PWA5tO__zVA6kpnyzkyvRoqWTqNLA5M6leNRLxBd4ny-Of3ZQ_50_rEghD9awVxTmSAfq3QCgJk0LEDb2u4MbSc97eQmyR-8w=w2400)

I was a big Madonna fan growing up and saw her in concert a couple times which was quite an experience. The Who’s That Girl World Tour at Anaheim Stadium was the first Madonna concert I had been to and when she beckoned the fans forward I was able to get to the first and second row of seats where we were all standing on the seats and smashed together and screaming and dancing away. I do not follow her more recent work but it definitely made an impression on me growing up.

## #8 - David Foster - Love Lights The World

![](images/kRYmKQPw2ZXIofbuz9H__gzNXniLxunZCdq261xt1JuQpoL2qt0rgm8790eL-29CRWu5YqnQpzG0DJLur8w6x7BRxa7gHcwkTOroAqC7RJ1SCe8esOGy6Jtr2ZQSG34C0VMwzKr1bw=w2400)

David Foster’s career is still going strong from the 1970’s to this day. He is a phenomenal producer, composer, and arranger. He has worked with so many artists and is known in the music industry as the Hit Man as he has had so much success in producing hit songs. It is hard to pick just one album but for me my very favorite song is track 4 on this album entitled - Listen to the children. It is a sweet and gentle piano piece that I love.

## #9 - Janet Jackson's - Rhythm Nation 1814

![](images/m-nV1ePLEH0uFT6p_CuY-Sb3dk_-RzpwzubY225PViBwbwPxqC4f0pvf5F_Al_JHqDtGtSGbLATGnRwJIkbNs22fk_AtFevBWWfVfRu8NKyL5UBukB8cKgLSTix9nNzlTil9UGAETg=w2400)

I was a big Janet Jackson fan growing up as her music had such energy. This was a memorable album for me as I saw Janet in concert at the Forum in Los Angeles. What a performance! So much energy. I do not follow her more recent works but still listen to her old songs with fondness.

## #10 - The Best of Bond... James Bond

![](images/wsIetSna0jW4qJe4YSUAKT77tUw5OMDNhU3LxPMV4BmNXjMBsqCXpxODYZaFh5nPFh3sn8GTkrhInKjdUlPvStHzShivU8MopKvs7Xczt8XedtkE8BqFDfpLLTOtTfqww_92-n115g=w2400)

There is no way that I could complete a top ten album list without featuring music from James Bond. The theme songs and the movie soundtracks are an enduring favorite for me. I even like to listen to the soundtrack with all the dialogue and sound effects. I’m a bit strange that way. In particular Time To Get Out from Quantum of Solace is a favorite (opening car chase scene).

## What music influenced you growing up?

I love so many different types of music. I have playlists for music that I listened to when I was growing up, for working out, for when I want to listen to worship music, dance music, or soundtracks.
