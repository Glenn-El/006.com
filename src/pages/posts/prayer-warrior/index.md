> For our struggle is not against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the world forces of this darkness, against the spiritual forces of wickedness in the heavenly places.
> 
> (Ephesians 6:12)

The bible is clear that our lives are not merely a physical existance but rather includes an unseen spiritual realm where a battle is being waged between good and evil forces. Prayer is an essential discipline for a believer to grow in their relationship with the Lord. In this article we will journey from learning the basics about prayer to encouraging personal growth and maturity in expressing your faith through prayer.

**Grammar Stage of Prayer:** In the grammar stage, prayer is about learning the basic ‘language’ of prayer. This includes understanding the structure of prayers, the different types of prayers (such as praise, thanksgiving, confession, and intercession), and the appropriate times and contexts for each. It’s about memorizing prayers, understanding the words used, and the order in which they are said. It’s the stage where one learns the ‘rules’ of prayer.

**Logic Stage of Prayer:** In the logic stage, prayer becomes more about understanding and internalizing the principles and truths expressed in these prayers. It’s about reflecting on the meanings behind the words, connecting personal experiences with the themes of the prayers, and beginning to see the bigger picture of how individual prayers fit into the broader Christian narrative. It’s the stage where one begins to reason with the ‘rules’ learned in the grammar stage.

**Rhetoric Stage of Prayer:** In the rhetoric stage, prayer is about expressing these truths eloquently and persuasively in one’s own words. It’s about personalizing prayer, making it a genuine expression of one’s own faith, hopes, fears, and love. It’s about using prayer as a way to articulate personal faith and to connect with God on a deeper level. It’s the stage where one applies the ‘rules’ in a way that is both true to the tradition and authentic to oneself.

#### Who is supposed to pray?

We are all supposed to pray. In 1 Thessalonians 5:16-18 it states "Rejoice always; pray without ceasing; in everything give thanks, for this is God's will for you in Christ Jesus."

#### What is prayer?

Simple put prayer is communication with God. God wants a relationship with us.

#### When are you supposed to pray?

In the area of motivation the bible has a great deal to say. To start with God is incredibly interested in our motivations, the why behind what we do. The bible tells us that our motivation should not be for selfish gain or to seek the approval of men but rather our motivation should come from a desire to obey and honor God.

#### Where are you supposed to pray?

The following are key bible verses that are related to motivation. It is by no means exhaustive but gives you a starting point to learning more about what biblical motivation looks like.
