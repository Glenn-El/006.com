> I have fought the good fight, I have finished the course, I have kept the faith. In the future there is laid up for me the crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, will award to me on that day, and not only to me, but also to all who have loved His appearing.
> 
> 2 Timothy 4:7-8

The Lord has blessed me so much in my life already but I still have many other wishes and dreams that I am working towards. God willing they will come true as I pursue these dreams with the right heart and motivation.

The [Faith](https://glennkelly006.wordpress.com/category/faith/) section of the site offers some insight into my foolish beginnings, how I came to a saving faith in Jesus Christ, how I am growing, what I'm doing now, and some of my life long dreams that remain unfulfilled.

People sometimes call these dreams their "bucket list" but that is a morbid descriptor for activities that you are passionate about and are working towards making a reality. All my life I have dreamed of the great adventures I would go on and many of them by the grace of God I have been able to accomplish.

I start by making a plan and then go after it with everything I have. We all have dreams regardless of our age or situation. As long as your dreams are not against God's will and perfect plan for you there is hope. I know that I have seen in my life time and time again where the Lord has blessed my dream and it came true. Maybe not always in my timing or in the way I thought, but the Lord is always faithful and true.

I hope that this website will serve to encourage you to love the Lord will all your heart, soul, mind, and strength.

**Live and Love in the Lord!**
