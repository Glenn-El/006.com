> 
> 1 Crointhians 6:19-20

Growing up if you were like me you loved to play outside all day with your friends. It seemed like we had boundless energy and could never get enough of play. Even eating meals seemed like a burden or time wasted as the priority was on play, play, and more play!

I have always loved to work out and after finishing my Master's Degree I was able to clear enough time in my schedule to focus on fitness with renewed vigor. I took the following approach as I am a big dreamer at heart and also a meticulous organizer.

### Dream Big

First off I want to encourage you to write down your dreams, goals, and motivations for fitness. At least for me the dreaming part was easy as I can envision where I would like to be. There are many sources of inspiration throughout history and in the present day. I liked how one online resource put it. Ask yourself is your fitness goal to be:

### Set Goals

My goals were to be healthy, physically fit, including endurance, strength, speed, and flexibility. I do not aspire to be a professional bodybuilder but just wanted to improve on all dimensions of fitness. Some may want to lose weight, or run a marathon, or get ready for an upcoming wedding or to get into shape to go to the beach. What are your goals?

### List Out Motivators

I recommend that you list out your motivations and review them every so often to help you stick to the long term plan and journey towards improved fitness. As an example my top motivators are:

| \- Honoring the Lord with the body He has given me |
| --- |
| \- Health and longevity so I can serve and be there for my family |
| \- Attractiveness to my wife and leading by example for my kids |
| \- Exercising Self Discipline (a fruit of the Spirit)/Delayed Gratification |

### Start with Prayer

Put the Lord first and go to Him in prayer. Some things you might bring to the Lord in prayer:

- Ask for His will to be done

- That you have the right heart motivation to seek His glory

- Ask for His strength

- Ask for His endurance

- Ask for His wisdom

- Ask for His blessing

### Research

Start doing your own independent research into fitness and documenting your learnings. For me this was simple and I started with a pad of paper but then moved to a Google Sheet where I started pulling together the basics. I asked friends who are into fitness for their insights and resources they used to plan out their workouts. As you can imagine there is a lot of information available and much of it conflicting. I encourage you to start wading through all of the information out there and zero in on the basics as well as start a list of the advanced areas and approaches that you want to dig into more.

### Tracking Body Measurements & Workouts

Keep a log of your workout exercise repetitions and weights and on a regular basis measure your body weight (some do this daily and average it out weekly) and take key body measurements (monthly). Advanced tip: Start taking monthly progress photos to be able to see how you look over time.

### Reassess & Adjust

We are all on a constant journey of assessment and adjustment as you find exercises and diet approaches that work well for you and some that do not. Always look for new ways to mix things up and keep your body guessing. The key is consistency and not being discouraged as the results take time. In particular as you get older be careful to not push yourself too hard but be patient.
